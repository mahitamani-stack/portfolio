Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 July 2026

This site is Mahita Manikandan's personal portfolio. It doesn't sell anything, doesn't require an account, and doesn't have a comment or sign-up form. This page explains the little data that does pass through it.

What's collected

Basic, anonymised traffic analytics (pages visited, general location/device type, how you found the site) via Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager. This is used only to understand which pages get visited and improve the site — it isn't linked to your name or sold to anyone.

Cookies

Google Analytics sets a small number of cookies to distinguish visitors. No advertising or tracking cookies are used beyond this.

Third-party embeds

Some pages load content hosted elsewhere, each governed by that provider's own privacy policy:

  • Canva — embedded case-study decks
  • Cloudinary — hosted video and audio files
  • Google Fonts — web fonts

Contact

If you email mahita.mani@gmail.com, that email (and whatever you choose to include in it) is received like any normal email — nothing is added to a mailing list or shared with anyone else.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will change too.

This is a plain-language starting point for a small personal portfolio site, not legal advice. If this site starts collecting client data (forms, logins, payments), this page should be reviewed properly before that happens.
